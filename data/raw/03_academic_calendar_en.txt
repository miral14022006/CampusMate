# Academic Affairs: Academic Calendar & Holiday Schedule

## Semester Timeline & Working Days
The odd semester commences on 15th July 2024 and concludes on 30th November 2024. A minimum of 90 instructional working days will be completed before theory examinations.

The mid-semester internal examinations will be conducted between 15th September and 22nd September 2024 for all departments including CSE, IT, Mechanical, and Civil Engineering.

## Vacation and Declared Holidays
The college will observe official holidays on Independence Day (15th August), Raksha Bandhan (19th August), and Gandhi Jayanti (2nd October).

The annual Diwali vacation will be observed for two full weeks starting from 28th October to 10th November 2024. Regular classes will resume promptly on 11th November 2024.
