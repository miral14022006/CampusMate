# Examination Section Circular 2024-25

## Exam Registration & Form Submission Guidelines
All undergraduate and postgraduate students are hereby notified that the examination registration portal for the upcoming End Semester Winter Examination will be open from 1st October to 20th October 2024. 

Students must verify all enrolled course codes, branch details, and elective subjects before final submission of the exam form. 

Late submissions will incur a late fee of Rs 500 up to 25th October 2024, after which no examination forms will be entertained under any circumstances.

## Timetable and Hall Tickets
The final examination timetable will be officially released on the college student portal two weeks prior to the commencement of theory examinations. Hall tickets will be issued only to candidates with at least 75 percent minimum attendance across all enrolled subjects.

Remedial and supplementary examinations for students with ATKT backlogs will take place concurrently with regular semester examinations.
