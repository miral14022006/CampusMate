# Accounts Section Circular: Annual Fee Structure & Payment Notice

## Tuition and Semester Fee Structure
The annual tuition fee for BTech Computer Engineering, Information Technology, and Electronics Engineering is Rs 85,000 per academic year, payable in two equal semester installments of Rs 42,500 each. 

In addition to tuition fees, students must pay an annual laboratory fee of Rs 8,000, library caution deposit of Rs 3,000 (one-time refundable), and college development fees of Rs 5,000.

## Online Fee Payment Instructions
All fee payments must be executed strictly online through the college ERP portal (https://erp.college.edu) using NetBanking, Debit/Credit Card, or official UPI payment gateway. Cash and offline cheque payments are not accepted at the cashier counter. 

Students eligible for post-matric scholarships or MYSY scholarship schemes must submit their provisional scholarship approval sanction letters to the accounts section before the installment deadline.
